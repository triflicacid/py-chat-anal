MONTH BY MONTH
21 September 2025 to 7 October 2026

How this was written: by reading, not by a script. For each month I read an even
sample of about 130 to 170 of the longer messages, spread across every day, together
with the message counts and the words that set the month apart. For the turning
points (30 November, 1 and 3 March, late June, 25 July, 24 August, and 13 to 29
September) I read the longer messages from those days more closely. It is a reading
of a sample, so it can miss things said in messages I did not see. Quotes are exact.
"You" is triflicacid and "he" is megamoleys.

Each month ends with a short list of the messages that seemed to matter most. Those
were chosen by hand from the month's most emotionally loaded longer messages, about 35
candidates a month, together with what I had already read.

ACROSS THE YEAR

The charts show him initiating less from May and June. The messages put the start
earlier and give a reason, and the reason is there from the beginning. He tells you he
is asexual in October, a month before you get together. On the night of 30 November
you mention having told your mum "we'd probably not end up together cause of ur ace
thing". At Christmas you say you were "slightly worried about me feeling this way and
you being asexual". On 18 January, in the middle of the most intense month, he says "I
don't think I feel love or attraction int eh same way", alongside "I've never felt
this way about anyone ever".

So the conversation on 1 March is not new information. It is the same thing coming
back after the two most physical months: he does not want sex as often as you do, half
the time he just wants to be with you, and he finds it hard emotionally. You accept
that without pressure, and the explicit talk fades from there. Exams flatten him in
May, and from June his energy goes into the fursuit, the society and a wider circle of
friends, while yours goes into reaching him.

He does not turn cold at any point in what I read. He stays kind, engages properly
when you bring him something serious, and ends it in person. Your own messages after
the break-up describe him as having been in a hard position for weeks and not wanting
to hurt you. What changes over the year is less how he feels about you as a person
than what kind of relationship he could manage, and the friendship is still there in
the last week of the data.
